Move to Heal

An AI-powered home rehabilitation app that checks your exercise form, so every repetition helps you heal.

Watch our video Our story

Exercises done wrong at home go unnoticed

Physical therapy only works when the movement is right. At home, nobody is there to say that a shoulder is too high or a knee is out of line.

The idea for Move to Heal came from a team member’s grandmother. Her condition got worse when she did her exercises the wrong way at home. The team built the app so that no one has to guess.

“Doing physical therapy incorrectly at home is a serious issue that often goes unnoticed.”

“We wanted to create something that supports people on their healing journey.”

The Move to Heal team, in the MIT App Inventor Winners Spotlight

Three steps from watching to knowing

  1. 1

    Watch the 3D demo

    A 3D figure shows the exercise, one slow repetition at a time, with the hold times on screen.

  2. 2

    Do it in front of your phone

    The camera follows your body and draws a pose skeleton over you as you move.

  3. 3

    Get instant AI feedback

    Correct and wrong bars show how your form compares, while you are still in the movement.

Under the hood, PoseNet finds the joints in the camera image, and an AI classifier trained for each exercise judges the form. The whole app is built with MIT App Inventor.
